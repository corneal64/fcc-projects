The Guitar Shop Experience

Who We Are

Our Story

Founded in 2015, The Guitar Shop began as a small family-run store with a simple mission: to make quality instruments accessible to everyone. Today, we're proud to serve thousands of musicians across the country, from beginners picking up their first guitar to professionals touring the world.

What We Do

We curate a hand-picked selection of guitars from the world's finest manufacturers. Every instrument in our store is personally tested and set up by our team of experienced luthiers, ensuring it arrives in your hands ready to play. We also offer custom setups, repairs, and expert advice to help you get the most from your instrument.
